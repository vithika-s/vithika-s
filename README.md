# Vithika Salomi

Ph.D. candidate in Communication at Georgia State University and Presidential Fellow with GSU's Transcultural Conflict and Violence Initiative. Before my Ph.D., I spent seven years as a journalist with The Times of India and DNA.

My research examines how contested information, from misinformation to state-sponsored messaging, moves through digital media, and how people respond to it. My dissertation studies the framing of election misinformation and the role of descriptive social norms in correcting it.

**Open to research roles starting summer 2027** in academia, policy and think tanks, and industry (information integrity, trust & safety).

### Methods
- **Computational:** R (topic modeling, Analysis of Topic Model Networks, network analysis, text and visual analysis); Python (web scraping, API-based data collection)
- **Quantitative:** survey research, randomized and factorial experiments, regression, ANOVA, non-parametric tests
- **Tools:** Qualtrics, Prolific, SPSS, MS Excel
